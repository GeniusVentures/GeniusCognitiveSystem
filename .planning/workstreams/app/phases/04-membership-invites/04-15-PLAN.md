<objective>
Make invite records issuer-signed and issuer-attributable, and make redeem validate the scope, the signature, and the issuer's authority.

Purpose: closes CR-08 (VERIFICATION truth 29, MEMB-01). `InviteRecord` (gcs_chat.proto:270-283) carries `scope_id`, `role`, `wrapped_room_key`, `is_space`, `authenticator` — nothing names who issued it. The authenticator is an AES-GCM envelope keyed by HKDF(inviteKey), and every input to it is known to whoever holds the link. The record replicates on `Membership::kMembershipTopic`, a permissionless topic: any peer can write a well-formed record for an invite id it chose, put its own key in a link, and hand that link out. The bearer gate passes (the attacker minted the key), and the victim writes a member record granting `record.role()` — for a forged ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN record, that is an elevation with no issuer at all. Nothing in the record ties it to an authorized member of the scope.

Output: append-only `created_by`/`issuer_signature` fields; hex codec promoted to the crypto public surface (invariant 11 — no second hex implementation); a sign/verify pair on the injected seam; a canonical payload builder owned by the invite domain; signing at mint; at redeem, a scope-in-URL lookup, a scope-kind cross-check, a signature verification, an issuer-authority check whenever the scope resolves locally, a GUEST-only rule when it does not, and wrapped-room-key validation against the invite's scope; plus unit tests and one shared test payload builder. The real-signer multinode forged-record proof is split into 04-18 (revision gate: 04-15 keeps 3 tasks).
</objective>
