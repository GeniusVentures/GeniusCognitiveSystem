<objective>
Stamp the `creator` and `members_can_invite` fields on space/room records so the Super Admin guard (D-03/MEMB-03), the approval check (D-07), and the invite gate (D-08/MEMB-05) have a persisted authority to verify against.

Purpose: Research pitfalls P1/P2/P3 established that `SpaceRecord`/`RoomRecord` carry neither field today. This plan adds a wallet-address-injected `EntityStore` constructor and stamps `creator` on new spaces + standalone rooms (nested rooms leave creator empty — their Super Admin is the parent space's creator, P2) and `members_can_invite` on new spaces/rooms, with `UpdateSpace` preserving the stored creator.

Output: `gcs_entity_store.{hpp,cpp}` stamping logic plus `test_gcs_entities.cpp` coverage. The FFI wiring that passes the real address and command field lands in plan 04-05 (default parameters keep the tree buildable until then).
</objective>
